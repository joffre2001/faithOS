# FaithOS privacy policy draft

Effective date: **[add date before publication]**

FaithOS helps churches manage members, ministries, attendance, events, contributions, files, and communications. The church operating a FaithOS workspace is responsible for the personal data entered into that workspace. Oby Soft acts as the software provider.

FaithOS may process names, email addresses, phone numbers, CPF numbers, emergency-contact details, church membership and roles, attendance, contribution records, uploaded files, and ministry messages. This information is used only to provide, secure, support, and improve the church workspace.

FaithOS does not process PIX settlement. The app creates a PIX payment code using the church's configured key. Payment is completed through the member's bank. A member may separately record that they completed a payment; the church must reconcile it with its bank records.

Data is shared only with authorized users of the applicable church workspace and service providers required to operate FaithOS. Personal data is not sold. Production operators must document their hosting, email, backup, and monitoring providers before publishing this policy.

Users may request access, correction, export, or deletion through their church administrator or `obensonjoffre3@gmail.com`. Requests are handled according to applicable law, including Brazil's LGPD. Some financial or legal records may need to be retained for a required period.

FaithOS uses security controls including authenticated access, role-based permissions, HTTPS in production, password hashing, tenant isolation, and audit-oriented operational practices. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Contact: `obensonjoffre3@gmail.com`
