Delete your FaithOS account

You can request deletion of your FaithOS account and personal information. This page applies to accounts in the FaithOS app published by Oby Soft.

How to request deletion

  1. Email obensonjoffre3@gmail.com from the address associated with your account.
  2. Use the subject “FaithOS account deletion request”.
  3. Include your full name and church name. Do not include your password, reset code, full CPF, or banking information.
  4. FaithOS or your church administrator will verify the request before deletion to protect your account.
Request account deletion

What will be deleted

Your login account and personal profile information will be deleted or de-identified from active FaithOS systems after verification, subject to church instructions and applicable legal requirements.

What may be retained

Financial, donation, security, dispute, backup, or legally required records may be retained for the period required by law or legitimate compliance needs. When possible, retained records will be restricted or de-identified. Messages or files involving other users may also require controlled retention to preserve those users' records.

Deleting your account removes access to the church workspace and may not be reversible. Ask for a copy of your information before deletion if you need one.

Church workspace deletion

A church administrator requesting deletion of an entire workspace should contact support. Ownership and authorization must be verified before deleting organization-wide data.

Questions

Read the Privacy Policy or contact FaithOS Support.
