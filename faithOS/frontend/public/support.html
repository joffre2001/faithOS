FaithOS Support

We can help with account access, church setup, member records, ministries, files, PIX donation records, and other FaithOS features.

Contact support

Email us and include your church name, the page or module involved, what you expected, what happened, and the exact error message. Do not send passwords, reset codes, complete CPF numbers, banking passwords, or other secrets.

Account and security help

Use the password-reset option on the sign-in screen when you cannot access your account. Report suspected unauthorized access immediately. Church administrators manage user roles and access inside their own workspace.

PIX support

FaithOS generates a PIX code, but payment is completed by your bank. For payment status, duplicate transfers, refunds, or reconciliation, contact your church and bank. Never share a banking password or verification code with FaithOS support.
