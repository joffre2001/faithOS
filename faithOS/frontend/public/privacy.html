Privacy Policy

Effective August 22, 2026. FaithOS helps churches manage their community while respecting the privacy and security of every person represented in the system.

Who controls your information

The church operating your FaithOS workspace controls the personal information entered into that workspace. Oby Soft provides the FaithOS software and processes information only as needed to operate, secure, maintain, and support the service.

Information we process

Depending on how your church uses FaithOS, information may include names, email addresses, phone numbers, CPF numbers, emergency-contact details, church roles, ministry participation, attendance, contribution records, uploaded files, messages, and technical security information.

Why information is used

  • Provide and secure church-management features.
  • Authenticate accounts and enforce role-based access.
  • Support church communications, ministries, events, attendance, and recordkeeping.
  • Respond to support, security, correction, and deletion requests.
  • Meet legal, financial, fraud-prevention, and operational obligations.

PIX donations

FaithOS creates a PIX payment code using the configured church key. Payment is completed and settled through the member's bank. FaithOS may record a member's statement that payment was completed, but it does not independently move funds or guarantee bank settlement. Churches must reconcile donations with their bank records.

Sharing and retention

Information is available only to authorized users of the applicable church workspace and providers needed to operate FaithOS, such as hosting, storage, database, and email services. FaithOS does not sell personal information. Records are retained only while required for church operations, legitimate security needs, or applicable legal and financial obligations.

Security

FaithOS uses authenticated access, role-based permissions, church-data isolation, password hashing, HTTPS in production, restricted cookies, validation, and operational safeguards. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, portability, restriction, or deletion through your church administrator or the FaithOS support contact. Requests are handled under applicable law, including Brazil's LGPD. Some financial or legal records may be retained when the law requires it.

Children

Churches must obtain any consent legally required before entering information about children or adolescents and must limit access to authorized people.
